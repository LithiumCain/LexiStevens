Rider Policy

A discreet ride depends on a few clear rules. Please read them before you book. By requesting a ride, you agree to the following.

  1. Illegal drugs and underage alcohol

    I do not transport illegal drugs. No one under 21 may bring or drink alcohol in the vehicle.

  2. No open containers

    Open containers of alcohol are not permitted in the vehicle.

  3. No smoking or vaping

    Smoking or vaping of any kind is not permitted in the vehicle.

  4. Cleaning fee

    Vomiting or other messes in the vehicle carry a $300 cleaning fee.

  5. No-shows

    No-shows forfeit the $25 deposit. After one no-show, future bookings require the full fare paid in advance as a nonrefundable deposit. A second no-show results in a permanent ban from service.

I may refuse or end a ride if this policy is violated, or if safety is at risk.
