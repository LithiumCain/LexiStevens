ZLS Executive Concierge

Rider Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

ZLS Executive Concierge provides private chauffeur and personal concierge service in Austin, Texas, and surrounding areas. By requesting or accepting a booking, you agree to this Rider Policy on behalf of yourself and everyone in your party. Service is billed at $119 per hour, with a 30-minute minimum ($59.50). A $50 deposit is required to book. Additional trip terms, including mileage beyond 20 miles from The Domain and any quoted packages, are confirmed at booking.

  1. Payment

    Accepted methods of payment are Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay, Tap to Pay, and cash.

    • Cash is paid at pickup.
    • Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay, and Tap to Pay may be paid during the trip.

    The $50 booking deposit is arranged by text after you send a request, and is due when the reservation is accepted. Any remaining balance is due as arranged at booking, unless a different arrangement has been confirmed in writing (including by text).

  2. Cancellations

    Please cancel as soon as you know you will not need the reservation, by text or call to the number provided at booking.

    • If you cancel 24 hours or more before the scheduled pickup, the $50 deposit is refunded.
    • If you cancel less than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup, the $50 deposit is forfeited.

  3. Wait time and late arrival

    The chauffeur will wait 5 minutes past the scheduled pickup time, followed by a 5-minute grace period. If you are running late, please text or call as soon as you can so the chauffeur can hold the reservation when possible. If the rider has not arrived or made contact by 15 minutes after the scheduled pickup time, the reservation is treated as a no-show, and the no-show policy applies.

  4. No-shows

    A no-show occurs when the rider is not present for the scheduled pickup and has not arrived or made contact by 15 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. A no-show forfeits the $50 deposit.

    • After one no-show, any future booking requires the full fare to be paid in advance as a nonrefundable deposit.
    • A second no-show results in a permanent ban from ZLS Executive Concierge service.

  5. Vehicle rules

    To keep every trip safe, discreet, and professional, the following rules apply in the vehicle at all times:

    • Illegal drugs. Illegal drugs and controlled substances may not be brought into, possessed, used, or transported in the vehicle.
    • Alcohol and minors. No person under 21 years of age may bring, possess, or consume alcohol in the vehicle.
    • Open containers. Open containers of alcohol are not permitted in the vehicle. This is ZLS policy, and it applies even if Texas law would otherwise allow a passenger in certain vehicles for hire to possess an open container.
    • Smoking and vaping. Smoking and vaping of any kind are not permitted in the vehicle. This includes cigarettes, cigars, pipes, electronic cigarettes, and any other smoking or vaping device.

  6. Intoxication

    Riders may be transported after drinking, provided they follow this policy (including the rules on minors, open containers, and conduct) and can travel safely. The driver may refuse or end service if a rider appears too intoxicated to travel safely, or if their conduct puts the driver, the vehicle, or others at risk.

  7. Cleaning fee

    Riders are responsible for messes they or their guests cause. Vomiting or any other mess that requires cleaning of the vehicle will result in a $300 cleaning fee. The fee is in addition to the fare for the trip.

  8. Damage beyond cleaning

    Damage to the vehicle beyond ordinary wear and tear — including stains, burns, tears, broken parts, or other repair needs — will be billed to the client at the actual cost of professional repair or replacement. The $300 cleaning fee covers cleaning only; it does not limit charges for damage that cleaning cannot restore.

  9. Right to refuse or end service

    The driver may refuse a booking or end a trip in progress if this policy is violated, or if continuing would put the safety of the driver, a passenger, or others at risk. If a trip is ended for this reason, the rider and their party must exit the vehicle when it is safe to do so. The rider owes the full booked time, plus any applicable fees such as cleaning or damage. The $50 deposit is not refunded.

  10. Respectful conduct

    All passengers are expected to treat the driver and anyone else present with courtesy. Harassment, threats, abusive language, unwanted physical contact, and discriminatory conduct are not permitted. The driver may end the trip if a passenger’s behavior makes the ride unsafe or reasonably uncomfortable to continue.

  11. Pets and service animals

    Please note at booking if you will be traveling with a pet or a service dog, using the checkbox on the booking form. Advance notice helps the driver prepare for your party.

    Service animals are welcome. Under the Americans with Disabilities Act and Texas law, a service animal (generally a dog individually trained to do work or perform tasks for a person with a disability) may accompany a rider with a disability. No additional fee is charged for a service animal. Emotional support, comfort, or companion animals that are not trained service animals are treated as pets.

    Pets that are not service animals may be accommodated when noted at booking, at the driver’s discretion. The driver may decline a pet for reasons such as vehicle condition, allergies of a later client, or the animal’s behavior. Please use the booking-form checkbox, or mention a pet or service animal when you text to book.

    A service animal should be under the handler’s control. If an animal causes damage or a mess, the rider is responsible under the cleaning and damage rules in this policy. The driver is not required to care for or supervise an animal.

  12. Passenger limit and seat belts

    The number of passengers may not exceed the number of available seat belts in the vehicle. Every passenger must wear a seat belt while the vehicle is in motion, as required by law. The driver may refuse to depart until everyone is safely seated and belted.

  13. Child car seats

    Texas law generally requires a child younger than 8 years of age to be secured in a child passenger safety seat system, unless the child is taller than 4 feet 9 inches. The rider is responsible for providing and properly installing any required child seat. The driver does not supply child seats unless that has been confirmed in advance. If a child cannot be transported safely in accordance with the law, the driver may decline or end the trip.

  14. Minors

    A parent or legal guardian should make the booking when a passenger is under 18, including for recurring trips such as school runs. Unaccompanied minors will be transported only when the booking has been made by a parent or legal guardian and any pickup and drop-off instructions have been confirmed. The adult who books is responsible for ensuring this policy is followed.

  15. Lost items

    Please check the vehicle for personal belongings before you leave. ZLS is not responsible for items left behind, but will make a reasonable effort to contact you if something is found. Arrangements to pick up or return an item are at the rider’s expense. Found items will be held for 14 days. Items that remain unclaimed after that time may be disposed of or donated.

  16. Changes to this policy

    ZLS may update this Rider Policy from time to time. The version in effect when a reservation is confirmed applies to that reservation. The current policy will be posted on lexistevens.com.

By submitting a booking request, you confirm that you have read this Rider Policy and agree to it on behalf of yourself and every passenger in your party. Questions may be sent by text or call to 512.534.1855.
